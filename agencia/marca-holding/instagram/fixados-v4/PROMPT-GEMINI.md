## Prompt A · O Siq (recomendado; a mesma cena da versão com foto real)
Ultra-wide cinematic photograph, 21:9, highest resolution. A narrow canyon passage of tall warm sandstone walls in deep shadow on the left and right thirds; in the exact center the gap opens to a sunlit wall of warm golden stone, glowing. Soft volumetric light falls from above into the gap, fine dust in the air. The two dark walls frame the bright center like a doorway between two worlds. Palette: deep olive brown, umber, obsidian shadows, burnished gold light, nearly monochrome warm. Quiet luxury, contemplative, museum atmosphere. Medium-format film, fine grain, sharp detail. No people, no text, no letters, no carvings of faces, no logos, no symbols.

## Prompt B · O óculo (luz do Panteão)
Ultra-wide cinematic photograph, 21:9, highest resolution. Interior of a vast ancient domed hall of coffered stone, almost dark. A single round opening at the top lets in one solid beam of warm golden sunlight that falls diagonally and lands as a bright circle on the stone floor in the exact center of the frame. Dust visible in the beam. The left and right thirds are deep olive-brown shadow with faint stone texture. Palette: obsidian, olive brown, burnished gold. Quiet luxury, sacred, minimal. Medium-format film, fine grain. No people, no text, no statues, no logos, no symbols.

## Prompt C · Dois mares (horizonte)
Ultra-wide cinematic photograph, 21:9, highest resolution. A perfectly calm sea at the last minute of dusk, the horizon line exactly at mid-height across the whole frame. The sky and the water are two almost identical warm fields of olive-brown and dark umber, separated by a thin line of burnished gold light along the horizon, with a soft golden glow in the center. Mirror-still water, no waves, no boats, no birds, no sun disc. Minimal, silent, quiet luxury. Medium-format film, fine grain. No text, no logos, no people.
