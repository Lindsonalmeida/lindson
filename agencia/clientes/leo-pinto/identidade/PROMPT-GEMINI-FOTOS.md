```

IDENTITY (highest priority, 100% fidelity):
- Keep his face exactly as in the references: same face shape, jawline, chin, cheekbones, forehead and hairline, same eyes (shape, size, color, spacing, eyelids), same eyebrows, same nose (bridge, width, tip), same mouth and lips, same teeth and smile, same ears, same skin tone and undertone, same skin texture, moles, marks and natural expression lines.
- Same hair: color, length, texture, cut and volume. Same facial hair exactly as in the references (or clean-shaven if he is clean-shaven).
- Same age, same body build, same height proportions, same neck and shoulders.
- Do NOT beautify, slim, de-age, whiten teeth, change eye color, smooth the skin or make him look like a model. No face swap look. He must be instantly recognizable by people who know him.

WARDROBE:
- Plain royal blue polo shirt (cotton piqué, color close to #0E4DA4), short sleeves, collar neatly open with the top button undone, well ironed, natural folds, correct fit for his body. No logos, no text, no stripes, no prints.

POSE: arms crossed, confident and friendly, slight smile, looking straight into the camera, framed from mid-thigh up.

STUDIO AND LIGHT:
- Professional photo studio, seamless backdrop in warm off-white (#F3EFE8).
- Large softbox key light at 45° to the left, soft fill on the right, gentle rim light to separate him from the background. Even, flattering light, no harsh shadows, catchlights in both eyes.
- Natural skin with real pores and texture, true-to-life colors, accurate blue of the polo.

CAMERA:
- Shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm f/2.8 portrait lens, eye level, sharp focus on the eyes, everything on him in focus, background slightly soft.
- Ultra high resolution, photorealistic, editorial political portrait quality, 8K detail, no noise, no blur.

FORMAT: vertical 4:5 (1080x1350 or larger). Leave clean space above his head.

AVOID: any change to his face, cartoon or 3D look, plastic skin, extra fingers, distorted hands, logos, text, watermark, jewelry or watches that are not in the references, sunglasses, busy background.
```
